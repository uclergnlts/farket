## Description (1684/4000)

Farket is a calm development diary for parents of children aged 0 to 4.

Write down what you notice in a few seconds: the worrying and the wonderful. "Didn't turn when I called his name." "Pointed at the plane to show me." Pick a quick note for your child's age or use your own words.

After two weeks of notes, Farket shows which signs keep coming back:
• Signs that need attention: Few, Some or Many, with the reason for the level
• Which areas repeat (social connection, response to name, shared attention, speech, play, repetitive movements, sensory reactions, routines)
• A clear next step: keep noting, talk to your doctor at the next visit, or see a specialist

Take a clear, dated record to your doctor instead of trying to remember. The doctor summary is a one-page PDF you can share or print.
